Reschedule

Move or swap exams whenever you like, or tell the planner what changed. It repairs the plan with the fewest possible changes: exams that can stay keep their session, and students keep their room and seat unless the change forces a move. Every rule is re-checked before you save.

Sessions before that date have already happened: they're kept exactly as they were, and nothing is moved into them.

How the planner decides what to move

The repair is a minimal-perturbation problem, solved with lexicographic optimisation in CP-SAT. Each objective is minimised while the ones before it are held at their best value:

1. D₁ = Σₑ (nₑ + 10) · movedₑ    people disturbed (nₑ = students in exam e)
2. D₂ = Σₑ nₑ · |shiftₑ| (×2 if earlier) + student welfare    how far, and how kindly, the moved exams travel
3. Seating per session: unchanged ⇒ kept seat for seat; otherwise fewest rooms → most students in their old room → in their old seat
4. Invigilation: shortfall → duties changed → busiest person's load → Σₚ |P·loadₚ − total|

All hard rules (no clashes, daily limits, same-course separation, room capacity, your rules) are constraints, never traded off. If the change makes them impossible, the planner names the conflicting rules.
